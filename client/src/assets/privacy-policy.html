GhostList is a zero-knowledge app. Your data is encrypted on your device before it ever reaches our servers. We cannot read your lists, items, or messages — ever.

1. Who we are

GhostList is developed and operated by [DEVELOPER NAME]. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What data we collect

GhostList is designed to collect as little data as possible.

Data we never collect: the content of your lists, items, notes, or messages. All of this is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before transmission. Our servers only ever store ciphertext. We have no means to decrypt it.

Push notifications (optional): If you enable push notifications, your device token is transmitted to and stored on our servers via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC). This token is a technical identifier used solely to deliver notifications to your device. It does not identify you personally. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings.

Technical logs: Our servers record standard access logs (IP address, timestamp, request path) for security and operational purposes. These logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days and are not linked to any user identity.

3. Encryption & zero-knowledge

Your encryption key is generated on your device and never leaves it. When you share a list with another user, keys are exchanged via Elliptic-Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) through an encrypted relay. At no point does our server have access to any plaintext data or any encryption key.

4. Third-party services

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): Used for push notifications. Google may process device tokens in accordance with their own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We have entered into a Data Processing Agreement with Google.

5. Data storage & transfers

Encrypted data is stored on servers located in the European Union. If you use push notifications, your device token may be processed by Google on servers outside the EU, subject to standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data we hold about you (i.e. your device token, if applicable). To exercise these rights, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Since we cannot identify you from your encrypted data, requests concerning list content cannot be processed.

7. Children

GhostList is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
